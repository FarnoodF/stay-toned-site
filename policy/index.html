Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

StayToned is operated by Farnood Faraji ("we", "us", or "our") and provides a macro tracking app that uses AI to help you log meals. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

What StayToned does

StayToned lets you log meals from photos, descriptions, barcodes, or manual entry, review AI-generated nutrition estimates, edit those estimates, and track daily macro progress. Nutrition values are estimates and should not be treated as medical measurements or advice.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store identifiers and profile details needed to sign you in and sync your data, such as your email address and display name.
  • Meal and nutrition data. This includes meal photos you choose to upload, ingredient and macro entries you save, and edits you make to AI-generated estimates.
  • Targets and preferences. Daily macro targets, nutrition and fitness goals, units, theme settings, and preferences you choose to record. Nutrition planning preferences may include dietary patterns, allergies, intolerances, excluded or preferred foods, meal frequency, cooking time, equipment, budget and other planning constraints.
  • Subscription information. If you start a trial or subscription, Google Play and our subscription-management provider process purchase status and transaction identifiers so we can unlock and restore paid features. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Optional health data. If you connect Apple Health or Google Health Connect, we may read or write only the metrics you approve, such as weight or energy burned. You can revoke health sync from the app or your device settings.
  • Privacy-safe product analytics. If analytics are enabled, we record limited product events such as feature usage. Analytics do not include photos, raw nutrition values, health details, AI prompts, or provider payloads.
  • Support requests. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and request details.

How we use information

  • Provide meal logging, AI-assisted nutrition estimates, syncing, and progress tracking.
  • Authenticate your account and keep the service secure.
  • Store your entries and preferences so they are available across your devices.
  • Improve reliability and understand product usage through privacy-safe analytics.
  • Respond to export, deletion, and other support requests.

AI processing

When you request AI analysis of a meal photo, the image and limited context needed for estimation are sent to our server-side AI pipeline. Results are returned as editable estimates. We do not treat AI output as medical-grade measurement.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers only as needed to run StayToned, such as:

  • Cloud hosting, authentication, database, and storage providers.
  • AI providers used for server-side meal analysis.
  • Google Play and our subscription-management provider for trials, purchases, and entitlement status.
  • Sign-in providers you choose, such as Apple or Google.
  • Email or support tooling used to handle account requests.

These providers process data on our behalf under contractual and security obligations.

Optional assistant connections and phone shortcuts

Connecting an assistant is optional. Before a named assistant such as ChatGPT receives access, you sign in to StayToned and approve its read-only connection in your browser. The basic connection includes meal summaries and dates, ingredient names and amounts, recorded nutrient estimates and their sources, consumed portions, macro targets, current nutrition and fitness goals, and dietary or planning preferences you have confirmed. Goals and confirmed preferences do not require a second approval. Missing or unconfirmed preferences are reported as unknown, not inferred from your meals.

Body and energy sharing are separate, optional choices. Body sharing allows recorded age and its reference date, height, current recorded weight and timestamped weight history. Energy sharing allows recorded burned energy from the health source you selected, its actual day intervals, provider and freshness or availability information. Each choice starts unchecked when requested by the assistant. Declining either choice leaves basic meal and context access available. Existing connections do not gain these optional permissions automatically. Device health permission and assistant sharing are separate: connecting Apple Health or Google Health Connect alone does not authorize an assistant.

These tools let the assistant answer your requests using your own records, including meal searches, target progress and bounded daily or date-range summaries. Responses may include meal and ingredient identifiers needed to retrieve details, dates, update timestamps, sources and missing-data indicators. The assistant cannot change your records. It does not receive meal photos, private meal descriptions, raw AI responses, billing details, account email, device identifiers or authentication credentials through these tools. Do not enter medical records or other unrelated sensitive information in meal labels or planning preferences. StayToned does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or verify that a meal is safe for an allergy. Nutrition and activity values are estimates and may be incomplete.

Approved responses are transmitted to the assistant provider you selected, such as OpenAI for ChatGPT, to answer your requests. This is distinct from StayToned's own meal-analysis processing. The assistant provider's privacy policy and your account settings govern how it retains and uses the information it receives. Review those policies before connecting. We do not sell this information or use assistant sharing to provide advertising.

You can revoke a connection in Settings → Privacy → Connected assistants. Revocation stops subsequent requests immediately but does not erase information already received by the assistant. Delete that information through the assistant provider's controls or contact that provider. To change sharing categories, reconnect and review the choices again; refreshing a connection does not add permissions. You can edit or remove recorded goals and preferences in StayToned, subject to the controls available in your app version.

We retain the named connection, approved categories, creation and revocation timestamps, and hashed authorization records while your StayToned account exists to enforce access, expiration and revocation. Authorization codes expire after five minutes, access tokens after one hour, and refresh-token families after 30 days. Expiration prevents access; it does not itself delete the associated database records. Account deletion removes those account-linked records. Our integration logs do not include OAuth credentials, search text, private descriptions, meal values or health values. Request limiting stores a counter and time window for each account/client connection, not request contents. Hosting providers may process request and network metadata for security and service operation under their own retention practices; these are not included in the meal results sent to assistants.

Phone shortcuts open the normal search or meal-entry screens. They do not automatically capture, analyze, or save meals. Optional dictated or typed descriptions are handled by the system service you choose and remain editable in StayToned. Shortcut labels and results do not disclose your meal history.

Data retention

We keep account, meal, target, preference and saved health data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. If you request account deletion, we handle the request after verifying account ownership and delete or de-identify personal data unless we must retain limited information for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn privacy-safe analytics on or off in the app.
  • Revoke Apple Health or other health sync permissions from the app or your device settings.
  • Revoke connected assistants in Settings → Privacy → Connected assistants.
  • Request an export of your account data from the app.
  • Request account deletion from the app or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Contact us at me@farnood.tech for help with privacy requests.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted transport. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

StayToned is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

International users

StayToned may process and store information in countries where we or our service providers operate. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by other reasonable means.

Contact

For privacy questions, export requests, or deletion requests, use the contact page or email me@farnood.tech.
